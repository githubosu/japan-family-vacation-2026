Tsukiji outer market (Nov 8)

  • Tamagoyaki on a stick — sweet vs savory, get both
  • Sashimi rice bowls: uni, tuna, salmon roe
  • Grilled unagi skewers + Hokkaido scallops

Tokyo

  • Nemuro Hanamaru conveyor sushi (KITTE Marunouchi)
  • Ramen: Ichiran · Kagari (Ginza) · Afuri
  • Gyukatsu Motomura · Tonkatsu Maisen · Sukiyaki Imahan (Asakusa)
  • Monjayaki in Tsukishima (Nov 8 dinner)

Kyoto & Osaka

  • Nishiki Market: tako tamago, yuba doughnuts, uni
  • Kuromon Market: uni on rice, oysters, snow crab, otoro at Maguroya Kurogin
  • Dotonbori: takoyaki + kushikatsu (no double-dipping!)
  • Kobe beef lunch in Kobe — Steak Land or Bifteck Kawamura (a third of dinner price)
